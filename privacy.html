Privacy Policy

How we handle information collected through this website.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This is a general privacy policy for the Mendel Info Labs marketing website. For questions about your information, or requests relating to it, email info@mendelinfolabs.com.

Overview

This policy describes how Mendel Info Labs LLC (“Mendel Info Labs,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with the website at mendelinfolabs.com (the “Site”). The Site is an informational website about our studio and products. It does not host user accounts, and it does not sell products or services directly.

Information we collect

The Site itself does not ask you to create an account or fill out forms, and we do not collect personal information from you automatically for marketing or advertising purposes. Information reaches us in only two ways:

  • Information you send us. If you email us — for example, using the contact address on the Site — we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, and we retain that correspondence to respond to and follow up on your inquiry.
  • Standard server logs. Our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, as part of operating and securing the Site. We use this only for reliability and security.

Cookies and tracking

This Site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts.

Third-party services

The Site relies on a small number of third parties to operate. These providers process limited technical data under their own privacy policies:

  • Hosting. The Site is served by our hosting provider, which processes the standard server-log information described above.
  • Fonts. Web fonts are delivered by Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address in order to serve the font files.
  • Linked product sites. Links to our products (such as onenew.ai and actpass.org) and to profiles on services like LinkedIn lead to separate websites governed by their own privacy policies.

How we use information

We use the limited information we receive to respond to inquiries, to operate and secure the Site, and to comply with our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except as needed to provide the services above or as required by law.

Data retention and your choices

We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to address your inquiry and for our ordinary business records. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have sent us by emailing info@mendelinfolabs.com, and we will respond consistent with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@mendelinfolabs.com.
